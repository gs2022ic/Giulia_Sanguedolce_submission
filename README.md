# HelloSelf · AI Engineer — Take-Home Task

**A better moment between sessions**

Thanks for taking the time to interview with us. This task is deliberately small in
scope. We're far more interested in your judgement and how you reason than in a polished
artefact.

In short: using a HelloSelf member account you register yourself, find **one specific
moment** in the app that could better help members between and beyond their therapy
sessions — and build a working web-based version of something better. You'll submit it as
a **pull request**.

---

## About HelloSelf

HelloSelf is a digital therapy service. We match members with qualified therapists for
one-to-one therapy, and we support that relationship with **Companion**, an AI that works
with both the therapist and the member. Companion isn't a standalone chatbot bolted onto
an app; it sits inside the therapeutic relationship and is trusted by both sides of it.

## The problem space

A therapy session is roughly an hour, perhaps once a week or once a fortnight. That means
the real work of getting better happens in the other ~167 hours, out in someone's
day-to-day life. People improve by practising what they learned in session, in real
situations, and by building towards the point where they can reliably use those skills
whenever they need them.

Today, that between-session time is mostly unsupported. Members are left to remember,
apply and self-correct alone, and therapists often walk into the next session with little
visibility of what actually happened in between.

## Your task

### 1. Explore the app and find one specific moment

Register your own member account and explore the app on **mobile** (the mobile experience
has the most to work with). A screen, a flow, an interaction — find something that feels
like a missed opportunity to help a member between or beyond their sessions.

We're deliberately not telling you where to look: spotting the right moment is part of
what we're assessing.

> **Getting the app:**
> - iOS: https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/helloself-therapy-coaching/id1515004784
> - Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.helloself.mobilemember&hl=en_GB
>
> Register a new member account in the app to get started.

### 2. Build a working web-based version of your improvement

Build a **web-based prototype** of your improvement. It should clearly trace back to the
specific moment you found in the app.

- It needs to actually run — but it does **not** need to be full-stack or production-ready.
  Mock the data, stub the backend, hard-code what you need. A prototype that demonstrates
  the thinking is exactly right.
- Use any language, framework or AI tooling you like. How you choose to structure it is
  part of what we're looking at.
- We're not testing your ability to work without AI; we're testing what you do with it.

### 3. Be ready to explain

Why this moment over others, what you built and left out, and how you'd know if it was
actually working for members.

## How to submit

1. Click **"Use this template"** at the top of this repo to create your own copy.
2. Build your prototype in that copy, on a branch.
3. Open a **pull request** in your repo. The PR description is where your narrative lives.
4. Share the link to your repo (and PR) with **@muchappreciated** and **@asmt3**.

**In the pull request, include a short narrative** (a few paragraphs is plenty) covering:

- **The moment you chose and why** — what you saw in the app that made it worth building.
- **Key decisions and trade-offs** — including what you deliberately left out, and why.
- **How you'd measure success** — how you'd know this is helping a *member*, not just
  that the feature works as software.
- **What you'd do next** with more time.
- **One thing your AI tooling got wrong**, or a suggestion from it you overrode — and why.

## Scope & effort

Please keep this to **no more than about a couple of hours** in total. We'd much rather
see something small and sharp than broad and half-finished.

## What we care about

- **User need over instructions:** did you find your own reason for this being worth
  building, or does it feel like a generic "make it nicer" pass?
- **Judgement on scope:** did you pick something specific and finishable, or something too
  big to actually validate in the time given?
- **Technical taste:** is the build itself sound, and does any AI/tooling you used earn its
  place rather than being used because it was available?
- **Outcome thinking:** how would you know if this was actually working for a member, not
  just working as software?

## A note on using AI

We build with AI every day and you're very welcome to use it here; it's part of how modern
engineering teams work. What we're assessing is the part AI can't do for you: your
judgement about which moment matters, your prioritisation, and your reasoning. Please be
ready to explain every decision as genuinely your own.

## The session

You'll present this in a 60-minute session:

- **15 minutes** — you walk us through your thinking and your build
- **30 minutes** — we dig into it together and explore some wider questions
- **15 minutes** — any questions you have for us

Present however shows your work best: screen-share, run the code live, talk to a few
slides. Come ready to explain and defend your decisions.

Any questions about the task, just reach out — we're happy to help. Good luck, and have fun
with it.
